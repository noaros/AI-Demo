# Personal Devices and Mobile Phones (IT-POL-006)

## Personal phones
You may use your personal iPhone or Android phone for Contoso email and Teams. You must install the Intune Company Portal app and enrol the phone. Enrolment creates a separate work profile. IT can see and wipe the work profile only, never your personal photos, messages or apps.

## Minimum requirements
Phones must run iOS 17 or later, or Android 13 or later. The phone must have a screen lock with at least a 6-digit PIN or biometrics. Jailbroken or rooted phones are blocked automatically.

## Personal computers
Personal laptops and desktops may not be used for Contoso work, except to access Outlook and Teams on the web through a browser. They may not connect to the VPN.

## Company phones
Company-paid phones are only issued to on-call engineers and staff whose role requires it. Your manager requests them in the IT portal.
