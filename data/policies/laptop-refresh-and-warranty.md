# Laptop Refresh and Warranty (IT-POL-003)

## Standard laptops
Standard employees receive a Dell Latitude 7450. Engineering and design staff receive a Dell Precision 5690. Mac laptops are available only with a manager-approved business justification.

## Refresh cycle
Laptops are replaced every 4 years. You will receive an email from IT about 30 days before your refresh date. Earlier replacement is only possible if the laptop fails and cannot be repaired.

## Warranty and repairs
All Contoso laptops have a 3-year Dell ProSupport warranty with next-business-day onsite repair. After 3 years, repairs are paid by your department's cost centre. Do not take Contoso laptops to third-party repair shops.

## Accidental damage
Accidental damage such as a cracked screen or liquid spill is covered once per laptop's lifetime. A second accidental-damage claim is charged to the department at the repair cost, up to a maximum of $400. Report damage by opening a hardware ticket in the IT portal.
