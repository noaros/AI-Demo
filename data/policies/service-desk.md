# Service Desk Contacts and Priorities (IT-POL-014)

## How to contact the Service Desk
The IT portal at `it.contoso.com` is the main way to open a ticket. You can also call extension 4357 (+1 425 555 4357 from outside), or visit the IT Bar on floor 2 of the Redmond office. The Service Desk is staffed Monday to Friday, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Pacific time. Outside these hours, phone calls go to an on-call engineer for Priority 1 issues only.

## Priorities and response times
- Priority 1: a whole team or a critical system cannot work. Response within 30 minutes, 24/7.
- Priority 2: one person cannot work at all. Response within 4 business hours.
- Priority 3: something is not working but there is a workaround. Response within 2 business days.
- Priority 4: requests and questions. Response within 5 business days.

## What to include in a ticket
Include your laptop's asset tag (the sticker starting with CTSO- on the bottom), what you were trying to do, the exact error message and a screenshot if possible.
