# Printing (IT-POL-008)

## Follow-me printing
All Contoso offices use follow-me printing. Print to the queue `Contoso-Print` from any office. Your job waits on the server until you badge in at any Contoso printer, then it prints there.

## Retention and defaults
Jobs that are not released within 24 hours are deleted. Printing defaults to double-sided black and white. Colour printing is available but charged to your department.

## Home printers
Contoso does not supply or support home printers. You may print non-confidential documents at home. Documents classified Confidential or Restricted (see IT-POL-011) must not be printed outside a Contoso office.
