# Lost or Stolen Devices (IT-POL-004)

## Report within 24 hours
If a Contoso laptop, phone or security key is lost or stolen, report it within 24 hours. Call the Service Desk on extension 4357 (+1 425 555 4357 from outside) or email `security@contoso.com`. This applies to personal phones that have Contoso email or Teams on them too.

## What IT does
IT will lock the device remotely and, for phones, wipe the Contoso work profile. Laptops are protected by BitLocker, so data on a lost laptop cannot be read without your credentials. IT will also reset your password as a precaution.

## Police reports
For stolen devices, file a police report and add the reference number to your ticket. A police reference is required for the replacement to be free of charge.

## Replacement
A replacement laptop is shipped within 3 business days. The first loss is replaced at no cost to your department. A second loss within 12 months is charged to the department.
