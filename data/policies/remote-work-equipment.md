# Remote Work Equipment (IT-POL-013)

## Home office allowance
Employees approved for remote or hybrid work receive a one-time home office allowance of $500. It can be used for a monitor, keyboard, mouse, headset or chair. Claim it through the expenses system within 6 months of your remote work approval, with receipts.

## Equipment IT provides
IT provides one docking station and one 27-inch monitor for home use on request, in addition to the allowance. Request them in the IT portal under "Hardware request". Delivery takes about 5 business days.

## Second monitors
In the office, every desk has two monitors. At home, a second monitor is not provided by IT; you may buy one with the home office allowance.

## Internet
Contoso does not pay for home internet. You need a connection of at least 25 Mbps download for video calls.
