# Leaving Contoso (IT-POL-010)

## Return of equipment
All Contoso equipment must be returned on or before your last working day: laptop, chargers, docking station, security keys and company phone. Remote employees receive a prepaid return box. Equipment not returned within 14 days is charged at its current value.

## Account access
Your account is disabled at 5 p.m. local time on your last working day. Your mailbox is kept for 30 days and your OneDrive files for 90 days. Your manager can request access to them in the IT portal during that time.

## Personal data
Copy any personal files off your laptop before your last day. Do not copy Contoso data to personal devices or accounts; this is monitored and reported to Legal.
