# Data Classification and USB Storage (IT-POL-011)

## Classification labels
Every Contoso document is labelled Public, General, Confidential or Restricted. New documents are labelled General by default. Customer personal data, salary information and unreleased financial results are Restricted.

## Sharing
Confidential documents may be shared only with named people inside Contoso. Restricted documents may not be shared outside Contoso at all and cannot be opened on personal devices. Sharing links to "Anyone" are disabled for Confidential and Restricted files.

## USB storage
USB storage devices are blocked on Contoso laptops by default: they can be read but not written to. If your role needs to write to USB drives, request an exception in the IT portal. Exceptions require manager and Security approval, are valid for 6 months, and only allow Contoso-issued encrypted drives (Kingston IronKey).
