# New Hire IT Setup (IT-POL-009)

## Before day one
Managers must submit the new-hire IT request at least 10 business days before the start date. Late requests may mean the new hire has no laptop on their first day.

## First day
New hires collect their laptop from the IT Bar on their first day. Remote new hires receive it by courier 2 business days before their start date. The temporary password is sent by text message to the personal phone number given to HR, and must be changed at first sign-in.

## First week
New hires must register Microsoft Authenticator on day one and complete the security awareness training within 7 days. Accounts of staff who have not completed the training after 30 days are restricted to email only until it is done.
