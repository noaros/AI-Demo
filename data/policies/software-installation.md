# Software Installation (IT-POL-005)

## Company Portal
Install approved software yourself from the Company Portal app on your Contoso laptop. It contains over 300 pre-approved applications, including Visual Studio Code, Zoom, Adobe Acrobat Reader and Python.

## Requesting new software
If an application is not in the Company Portal, request it in the IT portal under "Software request". Security reviews new software within 5 business days. Paid software also needs approval from your manager and a cost centre.

## Not allowed
Do not install software from outside the Company Portal, browser extensions that are not on the approved list, or personal cloud storage clients such as Dropbox or Google Drive. Use OneDrive for file storage.

## Licences
Licences for paid software are assigned to people, not laptops. When you change role or leave, your licences return to the pool.
