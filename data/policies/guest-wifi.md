# Wi-Fi in Contoso Offices (IT-POL-007)

## Networks
Contoso offices have two Wi-Fi networks. `Contoso-Corp` is for Contoso-managed laptops only and connects automatically using a certificate; you never type a password. `Contoso-Guest` is for visitors and personal devices.

## Guest access
Guest Wi-Fi requires a voucher. The host employee creates a voucher at the reception desk or at `guest.contoso.com`. A voucher is valid for 24 hours and for up to 3 devices. Guest Wi-Fi has internet access only and cannot reach internal systems.

## Personal devices
Employees' personal phones and laptops must use `Contoso-Guest`, not `Contoso-Corp`. Phones enrolled under IT-POL-006 still use the guest network.
